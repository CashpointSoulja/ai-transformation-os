Visual rules for the Product & Design build, taken from GitLab's Pajamas source, the @gitlab/ui design tokens, and measurements of the live gitlab.com interface. Read with DESIGN.md.

Logo

The official full-colour tanuki from @gitlab/svgs, unmodified. It sits top-left in a 48px bar at 24px tall, the way gitlab.com does it. Clear space is at least the x-height of the wordmark's "a".
